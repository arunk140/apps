A month calendar for the wrist. Today sits in an accent pill, and every day with an event gets a dot in its calendar's colour (up to three calendars). Turn the crown or tap **<** and **>** to change the month; tap the month's name to come back to today.

It can also live next to the watch face: long-press **Calendar** in Apps and choose **Add page** for a full-screen view of today and this week, or **Add widget** for a card with the date and today's event count.

Only dots and counts reach the watch, never event titles.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware), from 2026-11-15 or later for the page and the widget. Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the watch's app list (list style, not the waterfall).

Events need a companion that reads your calendar and sends the dots. It isn't released yet: without it the calendar works and shows "not synced".
